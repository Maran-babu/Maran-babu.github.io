Philopsophy

Biologically, we are the body, the cell, the mind, the neuron, the signals. My phiolosophy tells that the who we are, is not what we are. Our body is a collection of cells that work together to let us live, but we are something else, another concept that uses the body to interact with the world, and we have full control overselves, our expresions, and our actions. Thus, we must not be limited to our physical capabilties, rather, we are limited to the capabiltiy of our creativity, which does not have limit.
