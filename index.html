Who am I?

I am a student at Gretchen Whitney High School in ABCUSD with a passion for learning about STEM. I have taught classes to over 215 students across multiple different disciplines ranging from Math to Robotics to Oncology. I am also an avid tennis player, a member of Whitney's boy's Varisty Tennis team since freshmen year, as well as Team Mananger for Whitney's Girl's tennis team. I am a former top 350 ranked tennis player in the United States and won multiple state-level awards in the sport. Aside from academics, I love to hike and camp in national parks, with my favorite being in Yellowstone.

Purpose

While initially an assignment for my AP Computer Science class, this website serves as a blog where I can share my creations to the world. Unlike most people who post their arts, I for one can infamously not draw, however, I have a certain quirk for taking photos and writing papers and stories that encompass who we are and why we exist.
