Who am I?

I am also an avid tennis player, a member of Whitney's boy's Varisty Tennis team since freshmen year, as well as Team Mananger for Whitney's Girl's tennis team. Aside from academics, I love to hike and camp in national parks, with my favorite being in Yellowstone.
